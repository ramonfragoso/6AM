# 6AM

A 3D game built with React Three Fiber, WebGPU and Rapier physics.

## Language

Add each term here as it is introduced, with the words to _Avoid_.

- **Player**: the person playing, seen in first person. The camera sits at the Player's eye height. _Avoid_: character, hero, user, avatar.
- **Floor**: the walkable oak plank surface, made of the Kit's `floor` pieces, with its top at y = 0. _Avoid_: ground, plane, terrain.
- **Model**: a `.glb` file in `public/models`, exported from Blender and compressed (KTX2 textures, meshopt geometry). Load it with `useModel`. _Avoid_: asset, mesh file.
- **Kit**: the Model `public/models/kit.glb`, exported from the Blender collection "Modular Kit". Its Pieces snap together like bricks and share one set of textures. _Avoid_: tileset, asset pack.
- **Piece**: one reusable part of the Kit (walls `wall_2m`/`wall_4m`/`wall_6m` and `wall_end`, `column`, `column_corner`, `column_narrow`, `door_frame`, `drain`, `roof`, `roof_corner`, `floor`, `floor_tile_1`, `floor_tile_2`, `floor_division`, `painting_frame_1`/`_2`/`_3`, `square_table_set`, `round_table_set`, `chair`, `lantern`), a `prefab_<piece>` empty in Blender and a `kit_<piece>` node in the Model. Its origin sits at floor level; wall Pieces have it on the wall's centerline with their inner side facing +Z. Walls are placed only as column variants (`wall_4m__col_start`, `__col_end`, `__col_both`; the 2 m wall only as `wall_2m__col_both`, in the Corner) that differ only in their baked AO. _Avoid_: prefab (outside Blender), block, tile, module.
- **Placement**: one Piece put in the world: which Piece, its position and its rotation around Y. A layout is a list of Placements. _Avoid_: instance, spawn.
- **Corridor**: walls of 2, 4 or 6 m end to end with column pairs and Door Frames where its Corridor Layout asks. The House has two: the first runs from the fixed start door to the Corner (2.4 m wide, oak Floor, drains); the second runs from the Corner to the fixed end door (2.1 m wide, Floor Tiles, Narrow Columns). Built by `houseLayout`. Its left and right walls are named as seen walking from the start door toward the Corner. _Avoid_: hallway, hall, passage.
- **Corridor Layout**: the text describing one Corridor (wall lengths, `|` for a column pair, `F` for a Door Frame). The House's are fixed: `6 | 4 F 6` before the Corner and `6 | 4 | 6` after it. Doors are not part of it. _Avoid_: spec, pattern, map.
- **Corner**: the fixed right turn after the first Corridor: a 2 m square with Corner Columns on the turn side, a column pair on the far side, the 2.4 m end wall, `roof_corner` and a Floor Division across the opening. _Avoid_: junction, bend, turn piece.
- **Corner Column**: `column_corner`, a column with all four sides finished, used at the Corner. The one next to the end wall is `column_corner__far`, the same column with its own baked AO. **Narrow Column**: `column_narrow`, the same column turned 90°, used between walls in the second Corridor. _Avoid_: pillar.
- **Door Frame**: `door_frame`, the door Piece without the door leaf and knob, placed with `F`. _Avoid_: doorway, arch.
- **Door Wall**: the second Corridor's middle 4 m wall on the right, replaced by `wall_4m_door`, a plain plaster wall with a real doorway cut through it (Model `public/models/wall_4m_door.glb`), holding the Kit's Door Frame without its plaster door walls and a **Door Leaf** (leaf, knob, keyhole) that swings open around its hinge from Leva Door Wall `open`. _Avoid_: side door, door frame (that is the `F` Piece), opening.
- **Slam**: the Door Leaf hitting its frame shut from a small opening, speeding up until it stops dead, then bouncing back a little and rattling in the frame; it lands exactly on a slam in its Sound. _Avoid_: bang, hit, knock (for the whole slam).
- **Ledge**: the flat top of a wall's walnut panelling, about 1 m up, running along every Kit wall (the Door Wall has none); the top Candle Sets and some scattered bottles stand on it. _Avoid_: hand rest, shelf, wainscot (for the top).
- **Floor Division**: `floor_division`, the engraved strip between column pairs in the second Corridor and across the Corner opening. _Avoid_: threshold, grout strip.
- **Floor Tiles**: the second Corridor's checkerboard of single-tile Pieces, `floor_tile_1` (dark glass) and `floor_tile_2` (crystal), 4 across and about 0.4 m deep, each with a small random tilt, yaw and texture offset. _Avoid_: checker piece, slab.
- **Painting**: a framed picture hung on a Corridor wall: a Picture Frame Piece plus one painting texture from `public/models/paintings`, chosen per hung Painting so it can be swapped at runtime. _Avoid_: picture, artwork, image (for the hung object).
- **Painting Group**: Paintings hung together on one wall and moved as one from Leva: the two wide ones stacked on the first Corridor's left wall, the row of three tall ones on its right wall. _Avoid_: gallery, set, cluster.
- **Picture Frame**: `painting_frame_1`/`_2`/`_3`, the frame Pieces with their canvas; every Painting of a frame shares its size. Its origin is the center of its back, on the wall's surface. _Avoid_: frame (alone, it clashes with Door Frame), border.
- **Prop**: a Piece placed from Leva instead of by a Corridor Layout: the Table Sets and the Chair (moved, turned and sized freely, and solid) and the Lanterns. _Avoid_: decoration, object, asset.
- **Table Set**: one Piece holding a table with everything on it (books, bottles, candles), so moving or resizing it keeps the arrangement. `square_table_set` stands in the first Corridor, `round_table_set` in the second. _Avoid_: table group, still life.
- **Coat Rack**: the Model `public/models/coat_rack.glb` (Blender collection "coat_rack": a wooden rack with a cloth hanging on it and a bowler hat on top), a Prop moved, turned and sized from Leva Props › Coat Rack and solid through a cylinder collider on its pole. Its origin is on the Floor under the pole. _Avoid_: hat stand, hanger.
- **Chair**: `chair`, the wooden chair in the second Corridor. _Avoid_: seat.
- **Lantern**: `lantern`, hung on every column of the first Corridor and on one column of the second: its first column on the right wall (Leva Props › Tiled Lantern). Its origin is the column's origin, so it reuses the column's Placement. _Avoid_: lamp, sconce.
- **Crucifix**: the Model `public/models/crucifix.glb` (Blender collection "crucifix": a brass cross and the Christ figure), hung on the Corridor face of the Crucifix door's leaf; it swings with the door. Its origin is the center of the cross's back on the door's face, so `size` scales it in place; Leva Crucifix sets `height` and `size`. _Avoid_: cross (alone, it names only the brass part), statue, icon.
- **Clock**: the black alarm clock in the Model `public/models/clock.glb` (Blender collection "Clock alarm dekad"), standing on the Clock Table. Its **Hands** (hour, minute, second) step once a second from `clockTime`. It can count only one minute: the minute it is stuck in (`clockTime.stuckAt`). Its hour Hand shows the Hour; when a Loop's Passage ends, the Hand steps to the new Hour, the alarm rings and the minute starts again from :00:00. _Avoid_: alarm (alone), watch, timer, pointers (for the Hands).
- **Night**: one playthrough, from spawning in the Start Room at 12:00 until the ending after 6 AM. A page load or Play again starts a new one; its first Loop is always Clean. _Avoid_: game (for one playthrough), run, session, playthrough.
- **Hour**: the Player's progress, 0 to 6, shown only by the Clock's hour Hand: Hour 0 is midnight (the Hand at 12), Hour 6 is 6 AM. _Avoid_: level, stage, exit number.
- **Glitch**: the Clock's fault at the end of each minute: the second Hand reaches 12, the minute Hand jumps one minute, both stop for a second, then step back and forth fast at random for two seconds and snap back to the stuck minute and zero seconds. _Avoid_: bug, malfunction, reset (alone).
- **Clock Table**: the round table with the Clock on it, a linked copy of the Kit's small round table. It stands in the Safe Room the Player is in or entering and moves only out of sight, so the Wrap never shows it jump. Leva Clock › Table places it in Start Room space; Clock › On Table places the Clock on it. _Avoid_: nightstand, side table, clock set.
- **Lamp Shadows**: what keeps the Ceiling Lamp's light off a surface. The static ones are baked in Blender into the blue channel of each Safe Room part's occlusion texture (the doors, the lamp's own disc, so the ceiling above it stays dark); the Clock Table's is the **Furniture Shadow**, a small shadow texture the game draws again only when the table moves. Both multiply only the Lamp Light, so its color and intensity stay live. _Avoid_: lightmap, shadow map (alone).
- **Sound**: anything played through `src/sound`: a file in `public/sounds`, named in `SOUNDS` and played on a **Bus** (effects, ambience, music), either flat or from a **Sound Emitter**, a positional source that follows an object and fades with its own presence (the Clock's is the Safe Room's light level). _Avoid_: audio clip, SFX, track (for one Sound).
- **Fire**: the animated volumetric flame (THREE.Fire) burning on a candle or Lantern wick, placed at the Piece's `anchor_fire_*` Anchor. Each Fire has one **Flame Light**, a point light with static shadows. _Avoid_: flame tip, wick light.
- **Ambience**: the two background tracks that loop all the time on the ambience Bus, whatever the Player does. _Avoid_: music (that is its own Bus), soundtrack, background noise.
- **Fire Sound**: the crackle of burning Fires: one looping Sound Emitter per Lantern, for the candlestick, for the candle and one for the four Candle Sets together, heard only in the House. _Avoid_: candle sound, flame audio.
- **Candle Set**: a Piece (`candle_set_top_left`, `_top_right`, `_bottom_left`, `_bottom_right`) holding about 17 wax candles. The top ones stand on the `wall_bottom_details` ledge of a wall, the bottom ones on the Floor in front of it; all four are authored in one 2 m bay of the first Corridor. Each wick is a fire Anchor, and each Candle Set has one **Candle Set Light**, a point light whose world x and z are tuned from Leva; the bottom sets' lights cast static shadows, the top sets' do not. _Avoid_: candle group, candle cluster.
- **Anchor**: an empty named `anchor_*` inside a Piece that marks a spot for the game, exported as a `kit_<piece>__anchor_*` node. _Avoid_: socket, marker, locator.
- **Kit Surface**: the shading every Kit material gets in the game: baked AO, baked grime and world-space variation, with strengths in Leva. _Avoid_: shader, post effect.
- **Swatch**: one material baked by the export into a repeating texture tile (base color, ORM, normal). _Avoid_: atlas, texture set.
- **Walk**: the Player moving across the Floor (forward/back and strafing). _Avoid_: move, run.
- **Look**: turning the Player's view (yaw and pitch). _Avoid_: aim, rotate camera.
- **Head Bob**: the camera's rise, dip, sway and roll while the Player Walks, one dip per Step, following the distance Walked. _Avoid_: camera shake, wobble, view bob.
- **Breathing**: the camera's slow rise, tilt and drift while the Player stands still, fading in as the Head Bob fades out. _Avoid_: idle animation, sway (alone).
- **Step**: one footfall of the Player while Walking, at the lowest point of the Head Bob's dip; it plays a **Step Sound** for the Floor underfoot (oak or Floor Tiles). _Avoid_: footstep (in code), stride.
- **Creak**: the oak Floor groaning under a Step, now and then (about one Step in ten), never on Floor Tiles. _Avoid_: squeak, floor noise.
- **DebugPlayer**: a bodiless Player for inspecting the scene. It passes through everything and shows its coordinates in the bottom-right corner while viewed. _Avoid_: free camera, noclip, spectator, ghost.
- **Fly**: the DebugPlayer moving in any direction: forward follows its Look, plus straight up and down. _Avoid_: noclip, float.
- **Viewpoint**: which of the Player or DebugPlayer the camera sits in, picked in the Leva Debug folder. Only the viewed one reads input. _Avoid_: active camera, perspective, mode.
- **Fog**: the volumetric haze drawn over the scene by `PostProcessing`, thickest near the Floor, with patches that drift, tuned in Leva Fog. _Avoid_: mist, smoke, scene.fog.
- **Lens**: the projection applied to the finished picture (Rectilinear, the normal one, or a fisheye such as Equidistant, or Panini), tuned in Leva Lens. _Avoid_: fisheye (alone, it names only some Lens projections), distortion, warp.
- **Field of view**: the camera's vertical angle, in degrees, from Leva Camera `fov`. _Avoid_: zoom, FOV (in UI text).
- **Dust**: the small white specks floating in the air around the camera, each with its own brightness and a slow twinkle, tuned in Leva Dust. _Avoid_: particles (in UI text), motes, snow.
- **Underfloor**: the black plane just under the Floor that closes the gaps between Pieces so the Fog cannot show through them. _Avoid_: ground plane, shadow plane, floor cover.
- **Start door**: the House's door at the start of the first Corridor, between it and the Start Room; it opens from both sides. _Avoid_: front door, entrance.
- **Crucifix door**: the House's door at the end of the second Corridor, with the Crucifix on it; it opens only from the Corridor, into the End Room. _Avoid_: end door (in UI text), tiled corridor door.
- **Safe Room**: the 4 × 4 m tiled room behind each of the House's two doors, free of Fog and Dust. Whichever House door the Player walks through, they end up in the Start Room facing its door into the first Corridor, with a full wall behind them. The two look the same from inside: the **Start Room** behind the start door (the Player spawns there) and the **End Room** behind the Crucifix door (a second, never-opening door on its far wall). _Avoid_: saferoom, room (alone), spawn room.
- **Loop**: one trip from the Start Room out into the House and back into a Safe Room through either House door, going ahead through the Crucifix door or back through the start door, closed by the Wrap. A Loop holding a Haunt, Manifested or not, is passed by going back; a Clean Loop by going ahead. Falling into the Void fails it. Passing moves the Hour forward (at Hour 6 it brings Dawn), failing sets it to 0. _Avoid_: lap, cycle, run.
- **Ghost**: the pale, see-through figure of the Ghost Attack Haunt (Model `public/models/ghost.glb`): it waits against the wall beside the Coat Rack with its head hung, walks toward the Player once they come near and fades away when it reaches them. Its eyes glow and a blood cross marks its forehead and chest. _Avoid_: monster, zombie, enemy, character, the DebugPlayer (never call that a ghost).
- **Cockroach**: one of the bugs of the Cockroaches Haunt (Model `public/models/giant_cockroach.glb`): it crawls out of the crack where a wall meets the Floor, climbs a random height, then roams the Floor, walls and roof of both Corridors in short runs and sudden stops, sometimes trailing another, and scatters from the Player. _Avoid_: roach, bug, insect, critter.
- **Void**: the endless darkness behind the Crucifix door while the Void Haunt runs: the End Room is gone except the wall holding the door, and the **Void Floor** (two oak Floor Pieces) runs out from the door into nothing, with no Fog, Dust or walls around it. Walking off its edge is a **Fall**: the picture fades to black and the Player wakes in the Start Room with the Loop failed. _Avoid_: abyss, nothing, out of bounds, death.
- **Dawn**: the end of a Night, after passing the Loop at Hour 6: everything fades to black and Play again starts a new Night. _Avoid_: ending, win screen, game over.
- **Haunt**: one change to the House that the Player must notice; a Loop has at most one. Each Haunt is **Loud** (obvious, there to scare: lights die, a door flies open) or **Subtle** (a small change that needs attention: a Painting a little bigger, a Lantern missing). _Avoid_: anomaly, event, easy/hard (for its kind).
- **Armed**: a Haunt chosen for the Loop but not yet shown, waiting for its Trigger. _Avoid_: pending, queued, inactive.
- **Trigger**: what a Player does that makes an Armed Haunt Manifest (coming near the Door Wall, walking past a Painting Group). A Haunt without one Manifests as soon as it is chosen. _Avoid_: activator, condition, event.
- **Manifest**: a Haunt becoming visible or audible; from then on it drives the House. _Avoid_: trigger (for the showing), activate, spawn, play.
- **Haunt State**: the values a Haunt changes in the House (light levels, a door's angle, a Painting's size), each with its Clean value. Lights, Sounds and Props follow it without knowing which Haunt sets it. _Avoid_: context, haunt data, effects.
- **Caught**: a Haunt that Manifested in a Loop the Player passed. A Caught Haunt is not chosen again that Night. **Missed**: one that Manifested in a Loop the Player failed; it may come back. _Avoid_: found, solved, spotted, failed (for the Haunt).
- **Haunt Rule**: one named rule that, at each Wrap, looks at the Night so far and scales the odds of the next Loop being Loud, Subtle or Clean (×0 forbids, other factors nudge). The rules apply in order; the Haunt itself is then picked from the kind's unCaught Haunts. _Avoid_: policy, filter, constraint.
- **Clean Loop**: a Loop with no Haunt. _Avoid_: normal run, no-haunt.
- **Ceiling Lamp**: the lamp in the middle of each Safe Room's ceiling, whose round paper disc glows. Its **Lamp Light** is a point light just under the disc that lights the room the Player is in or entering, and fades out while they are in the Corridors. _Avoid_: ceiling fan, lamp (alone, it clashes with Lantern), chandelier.
- **Prompt**: the "Press E" text that fades in while the Player is near a door they can open and looking at it. _Avoid_: tooltip, hint, label, popup.
- **Passage**: the automatic walk through a door once the Player asks to open it: the camera lines up with the door, the door swings open away from the Player, the camera curves through and the door shuts behind. The Player has no control until it ends. _Avoid_: cutscene, animation, transition, door animation.
- **Wrap**: the unnoticed move of the Player once the door they came through has shut behind them: from the End Room to the same spot in the Start Room, or a half turn about the Start Room's center when they came in through the start door. Either way the door behind them seems to vanish. _Avoid_: teleport, respawn, reset.
